<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // How saturation is measured</div>

  <h2>One territory per marker, counted across the leaders</h2>

  <p>Each of the [17 brand perception markers](/concepts/brand-markers) has four territories. PokPok reads every leader's Amazon listing and places it in one territory per marker. A territory's **saturation** is the share of leaders standing in it: 9 of 15 listings in one territory is 60%.</p>

  | Saturation | Band | What it means |
  |---|---|---|
  | 80–100% | **SATURATED CORE** | Overcrowded. Standing out here is extremely hard. |
  | 55–79% | **MAINSTREAM PATH** | Well established. Limited room. |
  | 25–54% | **EMERGING NICHE** | A growing opportunity with moderate competition. |
  | 0–24% | **STRATEGIC GAP** | Wide-open territory, low competition. |

  <p>The **POKPOK Category Saturation Score** (0–100) sums up the whole category: for each marker it takes the saturation of the most crowded territory, then averages those 17 numbers. Higher means a more crowded category.</p>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // The scores per listing</div>

  <h2>Three numbers next to every leader</h2>

  | Score | Range | What it means |
  |---|---|---|
  | **HI score** | 0–100 | How the listing lands on people. A model reads it the way an impatient shopper would in the first 10 seconds, on five measures: Message Clarity, Trust, Voice Consistency, Audience Resonance, Decision Confidence. See [Scoring](/concepts/scoring) for the weights and bands. |
  | **Distinctiveness score** | 0–100 | 100 minus the average saturation of the territories the listing stands in. High = it holds ground its rivals do not hold. It describes a position; it is not advice to be different. No bands yet. |
  | **POKPOK score** | 0–100 | The HI score plus a bonus: 10% of how far the listing's distinctiveness sits above the category average. A bonus only, never a penalty, so it is never below the HI score. Leaders are ranked on it. |

  <Note>
    The listings report reads people's side of the shelf. The AI side of a category, built from the leaders' websites, belongs to Category Perception Truth (WWW), which is not yet available.
  </Note>
</div>
