## Who needs category intelligence?

You don't need to guess what the competitive landscape looks like. Category intelligence is built for three situations where guessing is expensive:

**Pre-launch brands** — Before you write a single piece of copy or settle on a brand voice, find out how crowded the territory already is. If 80% of your competitors are saying the same thing, you need to know that now — not after your launch campaign.

**Live brands on a growth plateau** — If your sales have stalled, your positioning might be the problem. Category intelligence shows you whether you've accidentally blended into the crowd and which territories are open for a sharper angle.

**Re-launching or repositioning brands** — When you're evolving the brand, you need a clear read on where the market has moved since your last positioning exercise. Analyse gives you a current-state snapshot, not a two-year-old competitor audit.

## How subscriptions work

Every Analyse subscription follows the same rhythm:

- **Monthly refresh** — Pokpok runs a new scan each month so your intelligence stays current as the category shifts.
- **15 category leaders scanned** — Each report is built from a fixed cohort of the most relevant competitors in your space.
- **You choose the category and competitors** — Specify exactly which 15 brands you want scanned, or let Pokpok auto-select the leaders for your category.
- **Delivered to your dashboard** — Your monthly report lands in your Pokpok account, ready to share with your team.

Each paid subscription is €50/month and can be cancelled at any time.
