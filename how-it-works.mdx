<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">04 // The 17 brand perception markers</div>

  <h2>Four groups, seventeen markers</h2>

  <p>Brand Perception Truth, Brand Alignment Truth and Category Perception Truth all use the same 17 markers. X-RAY uses 6 metrics instead, and the POKPOK Index uses momentum filters. More in [Brand Markers](/concepts/brand-markers).</p>

  | # | Group | Marker | What it reads |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | Foundation | Purpose | Why the brand exists beyond commercial gain |
  | 2 | Foundation | Mission & Vision | Where the brand is going and how it plans to get there |
  | 3 | Foundation | Values | The principles that guide brand decisions and behaviour |
  | 4 | Foundation | Positioning | The mental slot the brand claims in its category |
  | 5 | Identity | Personality | The human characteristics the brand projects |
  | 6 | Identity | Archetypes Mix | The roles the brand plays, from the 12 Jungian archetypes (e.g. Caregiver, Creator, Sage) |
  | 7 | Identity | Tone of Voice | How the brand speaks: language, register, rhythm |
  | 8 | Identity | Visual Identity | The visual cues that communicate brand character |
  | 9 | Communication | Messaging | The primary hook the brand leads with |
  | 10 | Communication | Narrative | The story the brand tells about where it comes from |
  | 11 | Communication | Key Selling Points | The evidence: who says it works, and what kind of proof it is |
  | 12 | Communication | USP | The unfair advantage the brand owns |
  | 13 | Communication | Focus | What the content is for: education or inspiration, trust or selling |
  | 14 | Benefits | Functional Benefits | The practical payoff the product delivers |
  | 15 | Benefits | Emotional Benefits | How the brand makes the customer feel |
  | 16 | Benefits | Self-Expression | What buying this brand says about the customer |
  | 17 | Benefits | Social Benefits | How the brand connects the customer to a group |
</div>
