  <h2>Three for people, three for machines</h2>

  | Code | Metric (as shown on the scan) | The question it answers |
  |---|---|---|
  | HI01 | **Message Clarity** [Relevance] | Can a visitor answer "what is this?" in 5 seconds? |
  | HI02 | **The B.S Meter** [Trust] | How many bold claims are backed by actual evidence? |
  | HI03 | **Voice Consistency** [TOV] | Is it the same personality from headline to call to action? |
  | AI01 | **AI Findability** [GEO] | Can crawlers and language models access, parse and cite your content? |
  | AI02 | **AI Signal Clarity** [Content] | Is the page dense with facts, or marketing filler that gets filtered out? |
  | AI03 | **AI Shelf** [Category] | Does AI file your brand in the right category, or with the wrong competitors? |

  Each metric scores 0–100 and carries a 2–3 word diagnosis label, for example FOGGY MESSAGING or INVISIBLE TO AI.

  | Score | Light | Reading |
  |---|---|---|
  | 70–100 | Green | The signal works |
  | 40–69 | Yellow | Weak signal, room to improve |
  | 0–39 | Red | The signal is broken |
