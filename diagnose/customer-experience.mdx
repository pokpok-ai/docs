Customer Experience Truth analyses 500 of your own Amazon reviews across 7 health zones and 17 pain points — turning raw customer feedback into a precise strategic map of what you deliver and what you don't. Instead of reading reviews one by one and guessing at patterns, you get a scored, structured breakdown of your customers' real experience: what they love, what they hate, and exactly which gaps your brand copy is currently hiding.

## What you get

<Accordion title="500 reviews analysed per report">
  Pokpok analyses 500 of your most recent Amazon reviews — enough to surface statistically meaningful patterns across all 7 health zones and all 17 pain points, rather than cherry-picked anecdotes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="7 health zones scored">
  Every review is mapped to one or more of the following health zones, and each zone receives a score:

  | Health zone | What it covers |
  |-------------|---------------|
  | **Product Performance** | Does the product do what it claims? |
  | **Delivery** | Shipping speed, condition on arrival, fulfilment accuracy |
  | **Packaging** | First impressions, unboxing experience, sustainability signals |
  | **Customer Service** | Resolution quality, responsiveness, post-purchase support |
  | **Value** | Price-to-performance perception and value-for-money signals |
  | **Trust** | Authenticity signals, consistency between marketing claims and reality |
  | **Emotional Response** | Delight, disappointment, surprise — the affective layer |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="17 pain points quantified and ranked">
  Within those 7 zones, 17 specific pain points are identified, quantified, and ranked by frequency and severity. You see not just what customers mention — but which issues come up most often and which ones drive the most negative sentiment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What customers love — the claims worth amplifying">
  The report identifies the specific language, benefits, and experiences your happiest customers describe. These are the claims your brand copy should be leading with — validated by real purchase behaviour, not guesswork.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What customers hate — the gaps your copy is hiding">
  The report surfaces the recurring frustrations, unmet expectations, and product gaps that your marketing language is currently papering over. These are the things you need to either fix or stop promising.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Strategic recommendations">
  Every finding is accompanied by a prioritised recommendation. Pain points are ranked by their impact on retention, so you know exactly which issues to fix first for the greatest effect on repeat purchase and review sentiment.
</Accordion>

## What to provide

The only input you need is your **Amazon ASIN**. Pokpok automatically retrieves and analyses 500 reviews from your product listing. You don't need to export or format any data yourself.

## How to use the report

Customer Experience Truth is actionable across multiple teams and use cases.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rewrite your product page copy">
    Use the "what customers love" findings to identify the specific language and benefits your happiest buyers use. Replace generic marketing claims with the vocabulary your customers actually respond to — it's already validated by real purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prioritise your next product iteration">
    Use the ranked pain point list to decide which product issues to address first. The report tells you which problems are most frequent and which drive the most negative sentiment — so your product team works on what actually moves the needle on retention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brief your ad creative team">
    Use the verbatim customer language from both the love and hate findings to brief your creative team. Ads that use real customer vocabulary consistently outperform ads written from the brand's internal perspective.
  </Step>
</Steps>
