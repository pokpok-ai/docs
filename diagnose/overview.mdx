## How one-off reports work

Every Diagnose report follows the same straightforward flow: you provide a URL or ASIN, Pokpok's engine analyses it, and you receive your interactive report within ~72 hours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Provide your input">
    Submit your brand's website URL or Amazon ASIN — depending on the report you've ordered. Pokpok automatically finds and scrapes the relevant pages; you don't need to provide raw content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pokpok analyses your brand">
    The engine evaluates your content through two lenses: Human perception (how real people read your brand) and AI perception (how large language models interpret and retrieve it). Every marker is scored and labelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your report">
    Within ~72 hours, your interactive report lands in your Pokpok dashboard. Scores, gap breakdowns, Uncomfortable Truths, and strategic recommendations are all there — ready to act on.
  </Step>
</Steps>
