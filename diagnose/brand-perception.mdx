Brand Perception Truth is a deep, paid analysis of a single URL — your homepage, campaign page, or any web property — evaluated across 17 brand markers through both the Human and AI perception lenses. Where X-RAY gives you 6 metrics in 60 seconds, Brand Perception Truth goes all the way: every marker scored, every gap named, and five strategic findings that tell you exactly what's costing you brand equity in an AI-mediated world.

## What you get

Your report contains everything you need to understand where your brand stands and what to do about it.

<Accordion title="HI Score and AI Score across all 17 markers">
  Every one of Pokpok's 17 brand markers is scored twice — once through the Human perception lens and once through the AI perception lens. You see both numbers side by side for every marker, so you know exactly where your human and AI legibility diverge.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Overall alignment score (0–100)">
  A single top-line number that reflects how well your brand communicates across both lenses. Think of it as your brand's total perception health score.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="5 Uncomfortable Truths">
  The five most strategically significant findings from your report — each one a named gap with a specific recommendation attached. These are the insights that are uncomfortable to hear and impossible to ignore.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Diagnosis labels per marker">
  Each marker where your brand shows a notable pattern gets an AI-generated label — a short, memorable name for the specific condition your brand has in that dimension. For example: "Poetic Fog Syndrome" (beautiful copy, zero substance) or "Category Ghost" (your page reads as generic as the rest of the market).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Blind-spot delta">
  The HI–AI gap broken down marker by marker. This is the core of the report: the specific markers where humans respond well but AI engines can't parse you — the exact places your brand is invisible to the systems that now mediate discovery.
</Accordion>

## What to provide

The only input you need to provide is your brand's website URL. A homepage works well — but you can submit any web property: a brand campaign page, a product category page, or a key landing page.

Pokpok's engine automatically scrapes your submitted URL and any key brand pages it links to. You don't need to supply raw copy, brand guidelines, or any additional materials.

## Sample findings

To give you a sense of what an Uncomfortable Truth looks like in practice, here are two examples drawn from a moisturiser category analysis.

<Accordion title='"The Personality Void"'>
  **Finding:** Your category is 80% clinical lab-coat tone. The same safety-and-dermatology language that every competitor uses has become invisible — to humans and to AI alike.

  **Opportunity:** Switching into Hype Beast territory (energy, culture, community) opens up a positioning space that only 20% of category leaders occupy — leaving 80% of the market fighting over the same clinical tone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title='"Everyone Heals, No One Transforms"'>
  **Finding:** Healing and repair language saturates the category. Transformation and empowerment language — the Empowerer territory — is used by just 7% of brands.

  **Opportunity:** Empowerer territory is wide open. A brand that speaks to transformation rather than treatment has almost no semantic competition in this category.
</Accordion>

<Note>

</Note>
