<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // Run your first scan</div>

  <h2>Five steps, no card, no setup</h2>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open the scanner">
      Go to [pokpok.ai/diagnose/xray](https://pokpok.ai/diagnose/xray). The counter at the top shows your free scans, for example **3/7 FREE SCANS**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Paste a URL">
      Your homepage, a product page or a competitor's page. X-RAY fetches that one page and reads only what is publicly visible on it.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Read your HI score">
      The Human score (HI) shows when the scan finishes. An AI model reads the page the way an impatient, sceptical first-time shopper would and scores three metrics: Message Clarity, The B.S Meter and Voice Consistency.

      | Score | Colour | What it means |
      |---|---|---|
      | 70–100 | 🟢 Green | The message lands |
      | 40–69 | 🟡 Yellow | Gaps a first-time visitor would notice |
      | 0–39 | 🔴 Red | People are likely leaving before they understand you |
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in to unlock the AI score">
      Until you sign in, the AI score shows as hidden with **SIGN IN TO UNLOCK**. The AI score comes from a model reading the same page the way a search crawler would, on three metrics: AI Findability, AI Signal Clarity and AI Shelf. Same 0–100 scale, same colours.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Read the gap">
      HI minus AI is your **perception gap**. Most brands score around 70 for people and around 40 for AI, so a gap of about 30 points is common. You also get a POKPOK X-RAY score (0–100) and, after the scan, the Roast: a deliberately brutal comedy take on your page.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</div>
