<div className="pok-lead">
  For every answer, three lists. **Mentions**: the brands the answer names. **Cited**: the pages the engine shows as its sources. **Found but not cited**: the pages the engine read for this answer and did not use. Together they say where the engine's opinion of a brand comes from.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // One real answer</div>

  <h2>Georganics, ChatGPT, October 2026</h2>

  <p>Question: <em>"What are good natural oral care products in the UK that are alternatives to Weleda?"</em></p>

  | List | What the engine did |
  |---|---|
  | **Found** (7 pages) | two Dentaly guides, a Mulberry Wholefoods category page, Truthpaste's plastic-free page, Kingfisher's About page, Ethical Consumer's toothpaste guide, and **Georganics' own page** <code>georganics.com/pages/fluoride-toothpaste</code> |
  | **Cited** | none: the answer carries no link |
  | **Mentions** | Georganics, Truthpaste, Kingfisher, Ben & Anna, Green People |
  | **Found but not cited** | all seven pages, Georganics' among them |

  <p>The engine read Georganics' page, named Georganics, and gave the buyer no link to it or to anyone. A visibility number says "named: yes" and stops. The lists say the page was read and not used as evidence, and name the six pages it was weighed against.</p>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Definitions</div>

  <h2>How each list is counted</h2>

  | List | Rule | Which engines give it |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Mentions** | A brand counts as named when its name, another of its names, or its website stands as a whole word in the answer text. Counted once per answer however often it repeats. | All four |
  | **Cited** | A page the engine attached to the answer: a link in the text, a source tied to a sentence, or a source card. | ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode; Google AI Overview by website |
  | **Found but not cited** | A page in the list of pages the engine read for this answer that is not among its cited pages. | ChatGPT returns its read list; Gemini and Google return only cited pages, so for them this list is empty by nature, not by measurement |

  <p>Counts for a brand come from the brand's own pages in each list: pages of its website, and pages whose cited text names the brand.</p>

  <Note>
    Ahrefs groups cited and background-read pages together as "Found in". PokPok keeps them apart: a cited page is evidence the engine stands behind; a page merely read is not. The IAB's standard calls for the same distinction (Citation Rate counts only linked or named references).
  </Note>
</div>
