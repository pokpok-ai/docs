<div className="pok-lead">
  When a buyer asks ChatGPT or Gemini a question, the engine **reads** pages, **cites** a few of them and **names** some brands. GEO is the work of being read, cited and named. PokPok measures all three, and shows where a brand's AI opinion comes from.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // How an AI opinion is formed</div>

  <h2>Read, cite, name</h2>

  <div className="pok-grid pok-grid--2">
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <div className="pok-tile__kicker">FOUND</div>
      <h3>Pages the engine read</h3>
      <p>While answering, the engine searches the web and opens pages. Most of them never appear in the answer. ChatGPT cites about one page in four of the pages it reads (PokPok, 10 days, 4 brands: 25–35%).</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <div className="pok-tile__kicker">CITED</div>
      <h3>Pages the engine shows as its sources</h3>
      <p>A link tied to a sentence of the answer, or a source card. This is the evidence the engine chose to stand behind.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <div className="pok-tile__kicker">MENTIONS</div>
      <h3>Brands the answer names</h3>
      <p>What the buyer actually reads. A brand can be named from a cited page, from a page the engine read and did not cite, or from nothing at all: the engine's memory.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <div className="pok-tile__kicker">FOUND BUT NOT CITED</div>
      <h3>Read, then left out</h3>
      <p>Your page was among the pages the engine read for this question, and the answer neither cites it nor names you from it. The engine saw you and chose someone else. This is the list an agency works from.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p className="pok-tile__note">The vocabulary follows the industry's: Ahrefs' "found but not cited", Trackerly's "in-line citation vs retrieved source", and the IAB's Mention Rate and Citation Rate (Measuring Visibility in the AI Era, August 2026).</p>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // The four P's</div>

  <h2>The IAB's hierarchy, and where PokPok measures each</h2>

  | IAB level | Question | Metric | PokPok |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Presence** | Does the brand appear? | Mention rate, citation rate, share of voice, momentum | AI Visibility Truth: visibility, share of voice, change versus the period before. Report: named in N of 16 answers |
  | **Prominence** | Where, and how prominently? | Position | Not yet available |
  | **Portrayal** | In what context, with what accuracy? | Framing, factual accuracy | Report: every answer word for word, the brands it names, how it describes yours |
  | **Persuasion** | Does visibility drive action? | Recommendation strength, click-through | Report: recommended in N of 16 answers |

  <p>Definitions and formulas: [GEO metrics](/geo/metrics). The three lists in detail: [Mentions, Cited, Found but not cited](/geo/mentions-cited-found).</p>
</div>
