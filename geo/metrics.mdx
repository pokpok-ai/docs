<div className="pok-lead">
  Each metric below carries the IAB's definition (Measuring Visibility in the AI Era, August 2026), PokPok's exact formula, and where it is live. **Read each number with its definition**: two tools can report different figures for the same brand because they count differently.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // Presence</div>

  <h2>Does the brand appear?</h2>

  | Metric | IAB definition | PokPok formula | Live |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Visibility** (Mention Rate) | "responses containing a brand mention divided by total responses" | answers naming the brand ÷ all answers, per engine and overall | AI Visibility Truth, report |
  | **Citation rate** | "responses containing a brand citation divided by total responses"; a citation is "a linked reference (containing a hyperlink) or a named reference" | answers citing a page of the brand's website ÷ all answers. Named references without a link are not yet counted | AI Visibility Truth: shown today as the brand's share of all citations; the IAB form is not yet available |
  | **Share of voice** | "brand mentions as a proportion of all brand mentions within a defined competitive category" | the brand's named answers ÷ named answers of the brand and its tracked competitors. The category is the competitor list the client sets | AI Visibility Truth |
  | **Visibility momentum** | "percentage change in Mention Rate or Share of Voice between defined measurement periods" | this period against the equal period before it | AI Visibility Truth |

  <Note>
    The IAB: "A brand can have a high Mention Rate and a low Citation Rate; the gap between the two is itself a meaningful signal." Toyota on ChatGPT, 10 days: named in 98.7% of answers, a page of toyota.com cited in 2.7%.
  </Note>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Prominence</div>

  <h2>Where, and how prominently?</h2>

  | Metric | IAB definition | PokPok formula | Live |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Position** | "where the brand appears in the formatted AI response as a user sees it … its rank when multiple brands are listed" | the brand's place among all brands the answer names, in reading order, counted on the whole answer; averaged over answers naming the brand. Win rate = answers where the brand comes first ÷ all answers | Not yet available |
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // Portrayal</div>

  <h2>In what context, with what accuracy?</h2>

  | Metric | IAB definition | PokPok | Live |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Factual accuracy** | claims about the brand checked against a brand-supplied source of truth | the brand's own page is the source of truth PokPok already holds | Not yet available |
  | **Every answer, word for word** | — | the report shows each answer as the engine gave it, the brands it names and how it describes yours | Report |
</div>
