## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run your free X-RAY scan">
    Visit [pokpok.ai](https://pokpok.ai) and paste any brand URL into the X-RAY scanner. You'll get your Human Perception (HI) score instantly — no sign-in needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your perception gaps">
    See exactly where your brand's human and AI scores diverge. A wide gap means AI models are forming a very different impression of your brand than real shoppers are — that's the blind spot Pokpok makes visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade to a full report">
    When you're ready to go deeper, order a Diagnose report for €150. You'll receive five Uncomfortable Truths about your brand, a full comparison against your category, and a prioritised fix list — delivered in ~72 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The X-RAY scanner is completely free. You get 3 anonymous scans, plus 1 more when you sign in with Google, plus 3 more when you share your result — **7 free scans in total**. No credit card required.
</Tip>
