Pokpok is a brand perception intelligence platform that tells you what your brand actually communicates — not what you intend it to. It measures your brand across two distinct lenses — how human shoppers read you and how AI models interpret you — then delivers a concrete, prioritised list of fixes so you can close the gap between the two.

## The Two Lenses

Every Pokpok analysis measures your brand through two complementary perspectives.

**HI — Human Perception**
The HI score reflects how clearly and compellingly your brand communicates to a real shopper. It draws on the same signals a consumer uses when they land on your page: your messaging, your product story, your tone of voice, your visual cues. A low HI score means real shoppers are bouncing before they understand why your product is for them.

**AI — AI Model Perception**
The AI score reflects how generative search engines — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and their peers — interpret and represent your brand when a consumer asks for a recommendation. These engines are rapidly becoming a primary discovery channel for DTC brands. If your AI score is low, your brand may be invisible, mischaracterised, or deprioritised in AI-generated answers, even if your HI score is strong.

The delta between your HI and AI scores is your **perception gap** — the blind spot that most brands don't know they have.
