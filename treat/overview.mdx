The Treat family closes the loop: once Pokpok has diagnosed your brand's perception gaps, it uses that exact diagnosis to rebuild your content. Every asset generated traces directly to a named pathology in your report. This is not a generic AI writing tool — it is a precision rebuild engine that uses your brand's specific failure modes as its brief.

## What's Coming

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Page Content Treatment" icon="file-lines">
    Rewrites your product page copy from the diagnosed gaps. If your report says "Selling The Data, Not The Feeling", the rewrite shifts from Derm-Validation to Sensory Experience language. Every sentence has a source — you can trace it back to the exact pathology it addresses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product Gallery Treatment" icon="images">
    Regenerates your product imagery from your visual identity gaps. If your Visual Identity marker is misaligned, the new gallery is built to bridge that gap. Each image is generated against a specific visual pathology, not a mood board.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why This Matters

Gap-traced content is fundamentally different from generic AI copy. Most AI content tools optimise for readability or brand voice guidelines — they have no idea what is actually broken on your page. Because every line Treat generates comes from a specific brand pathology in your Diagnose report, it closes real gaps rather than producing polished-but-ineffective copy. You know exactly why each sentence was written, and you can measure whether it works.

## How the Chain Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run a Diagnose report">
    Purchase a Brand Perception Truth or Brand Alignment Truth report for your product or brand. Pokpok scrapes and analyses your page within ~72 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your 5 Uncomfortable Truths">
    Your report surfaces the five most critical brand perception gaps, each named and prioritised. This becomes the foundation for your Treat brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commission a Treat project">
    Submit your Diagnose report as the brief for a Treat project. Pokpok uses your named pathologies — not generic instructions — as the generation prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive rebuilt content">
    Get rebuilt copy and/or imagery delivered to your dashboard. Each asset is linked to the gap it closes, so you can audit the output against the diagnosis.
  </Step>
</Steps>
