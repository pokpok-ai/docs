<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // The two lenses</div>

  <h2>HI score and AI score: two scales</h2>

  <div className="pok-grid pok-grid--2">
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <div className="pok-tile__kicker">HI</div>
      <h3>Weighted: clarity and trust first</h3>
      <p>HI = 0.30 × Message Clarity + 0.25 × Trust Score + 0.15 × Voice Consistency + 0.15 × Audience Resonance + 0.15 × Decision Confidence.</p>
      <p className="pok-tile__note">A model reads the page as an impatient shopper in the first 10 seconds. If they cannot understand it, nothing else matters.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <div className="pok-tile__kicker">AI</div>
      <h3>Equal weights: five metrics</h3>
      <p>AI = average of AI FIND, AI READ, AI KNOW, AI TRUST and AI DO, 20% each. Each metric is the average of its five sub-measures.</p>
      <p className="pok-tile__note">AI Standing (is the brand known elsewhere?) is shown beside it as a band and is not part of the AI score.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p>Each HI metric gets a band label. Two of them as examples:</p>

  | Metric score | Message Clarity | Trust Score |
  |---|---|---|
  | 80–100 | INSTANT-READ | RECEIPTS LOUD |
  | 65–79 | UNDERSTANDABLE | CREDIBLE |
  | 50–64 | TAKES EFFORT | MIXED EVIDENCE |
  | 35–49 | OPAQUE | HYPE-HEAVY |
  | 20–34 | UNREADABLE | TRUST GAP |
  | 0–19 | WORD SOUP | NO RECEIPTS |

  <Note>
    The HI and AI scores measure different things, so the report shows each one on its own, side by side. The headline POKPOK Brand Perception Score weighs them 60/40 (0.6 × HI + 0.4 × AI). The five AI metrics are separate from the 17 brand perception markers.
  </Note>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // The 60/40 marker alignment</div>

  <h2>Do two reads of a marker tell the same story?</h2>

  <p>The same formula compares two reads of each of the [17 markers](/concepts/brand-markers). In **Brand Perception Truth (Single URL) -geo** the two reads are people's and ChatGPT's. In **Brand Alignment Truth** they are your website and your Amazon product page.</p>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Territory match: 60 points">
      Same territory in both reads → 60 points. Different territory → 0. A territory mismatch costs 60 of the marker's 100 points.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Slider proximity: 40 points">
      Two sliders worth 20 points each. Same position → 20, one step apart → 10, further apart → 0.
      Tone of Voice: four sliders worth 10 each, same rule. Archetypes Mix: primary, secondary and tertiary archetype worth 20, 10 and 10; an archetype present at a different rank gets half.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Marker score">
      Territory points + slider points, 0–100%. The arithmetic: same territory, one slider identical, one slider two steps apart = 60 + 20 + 0 = **80%**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Overall">
      Brand Alignment Truth: the sum of the 17 marker scores ÷ 17. The -geo report: the average over the markers both reads cover; markers one read did not cover are counted and shown, not averaged in.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  | Alignment | Band |
  |---|---|
  | 90–100% | **Healthy** |
  | 70–89% | **Stable** |
  | 50–69% | **Guarded** |
  | 0–49% | **Critical** |
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">04 // Category and quick-check scores</div>

  <h2>Saturation, the Index and the X-RAY lights</h2>

  **Saturation %** is the share of category leaders standing in one territory of a marker. The **POKPOK Category Saturation Score** (0–100) averages, over the 17 markers, the saturation of each marker's most crowded territory.

  | Saturation | Band |
  |---|---|
  | 80–100% | **SATURATED CORE** |
  | 55–79% | **MAINSTREAM PATH** |
  | 25–54% | **EMERGING NICHE** |
  | 0–24% | **STRATEGIC GAP** |

  **X-RAY lights** cover 6 metrics (HI01–HI03, AI01–AI03): **Green** 70–100 · **Orange** 40–69 · **Red** 0–39.

  The **POKPOK Index score** has nine bands, from UNSTOPPABLE FORCE (90–100) to FLATLINE (0–19): see [POKPOK Index](/analyse/pokpok-index).
</div>
