<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // The core: 17 markers on both lenses</div>

  <h2>Does AI describe you the way people read you?</h2>

  <p>The five AI metrics test whether a machine can use your page. They are a separate instrument. The question that matters more is whether AI tells your brand's story the way people read it: the same purpose, the same personality, the same proof.</p>

  <p>In the -geo report, ChatGPT reads your brand on all [17 brand perception markers](/concepts/brand-markers), next to the people read. Each marker gets an alignment % from the 60/40 formula, with a band:</p>

  | Alignment | Band |
  |---|---|
  | 90–100% | **Healthy** |
  | 70–89% | **Stable** |
  | 50–69% | **Guarded** |
  | 0–49% | **Critical** |

  <Tip>
    Start with the Critical markers: that is where AI tells a different story about you than your page tells people.
  </Tip>
</div>
