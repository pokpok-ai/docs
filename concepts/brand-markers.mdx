<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // Pick the comparison you need</div>

  <h2>Each report reads the markers against something different</h2>

  | Report | What the markers compare | Score and bands |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Brand Perception Truth** | People's read vs ChatGPT's read of the same page | Alignment % per marker: Healthy 90–100 · Stable 70–89 · Guarded 50–69 · Critical 0–49 |
  | **Brand Alignment Truth** | Your website vs your Amazon product page | Same 60/40 formula and bands; a split between the two is a **Multiple Personality Disorder** finding |
  | **Category Perception Truth (PDP)** | The category leaders' Amazon listings against each other | Saturation % per territory: SATURATED CORE 80–100 · MAINSTREAM PATH 55–79 · EMERGING NICHE 25–54 · STRATEGIC GAP 0–24 |
  | **Category Perception Truth (WWW)** | The category leaders' websites against each other | Same saturation bands |

  <p>In the category reports every leader also gets a **distinctiveness score** (0–100): 100 minus the average saturation of the territories it stands in. High means it holds ground its rivals do not hold. See [Category Perception](/analyse/category-perception).</p>

  <p>How the 60/40 alignment and saturation are calculated: [Scoring](/concepts/scoring).</p>

  <Note>
    The five AI metrics (AI FIND, READ, KNOW, TRUST, DO) are a separate instrument. They test whether a machine can use your page; the 17 markers read what your brand means.
  </Note>
</div>
