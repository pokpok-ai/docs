<div className="pok-lead">
  One X-RAY scan of a new URL costs one credit. You get 3 without an account, and sign-in and one LinkedIn share take you to the 7-credit maximum. No payment, ever.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // How you get credits</div>

  <h2>3 to start, 7 at most</h2>

  | Event | Credits | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Open the scanner | 3 | No account needed; tracked in this browser |
  | Sign in | +1 | Once per account; the scanner's **SIGN IN +1** button opens Google sign-in |
  | Share your result | +3 | Once per account, signed in only; opens LinkedIn's own share dialog |
  | **Maximum** | **7** | No bonus takes your balance above 7 |

  <p className="pok-tile__note">Not signed in yet? Your scans are counted by a browser fingerprint. On an account's first sign-in, the scans left in that browser move to the account, plus 1. An account that already holds credits gets the +1 once; the browser's anonymous credits are not added to it.</p>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Repeat scans</div>

  <h2>A URL anyone has scanned before costs nothing</h2>

  <p>If that URL was scanned before, by you or anyone else, X-RAY returns the stored result and gives your credit back.</p>

  | Same URL? | Example |
  |---|---|
  | Yes | `https://Brand.com/` and `https://brand.com`, a `#section` added at the end |
  | No, a new scan | `brand.com/shop/` vs `brand.com/shop`, or any `?utm_source=…` added |

  <Note>
    A stored result is never refreshed. If you changed the page since its first scan, a repeat scan still shows the old result.
  </Note>
</div>
