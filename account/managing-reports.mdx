<div className="pok-lead">
  Every report you pay for appears on [pokpok.ai/my-reports](https://pokpok.ai/my-reports), newest first. Diagnose reports are delivered next day, and you get an email when yours is ready.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // Open your reports</div>

  <h2>Four steps</h2>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in">
      Go to [pokpok.ai/signin](https://pokpok.ai/signin) and sign in with the same account you ordered with: Google, Apple, or email and password.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Open My Reports">
      Go to [pokpok.ai/my-reports](https://pokpok.ai/my-reports). Your orders are listed newest first by payment date, under three tabs: **ALL ORDERS**, **Delivered** and **In progress**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the button on each order">
      The button on each order card tells you where the report is (see the table below).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Open the report">
      Press **VIEW REPORT** to open the full report. It stays in the list, so you can come back to it.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Order status</div>

  <h2>What the order card shows</h2>

  | Card shows | What it means | What to do |
  |---|---|---|
  | **ANALYZING…** with the current step, for example "Scanning homepage…", "Checking AI visibility…", "Synthesising 5 truths…" | The report is being made | Nothing: you get an email when it is ready |
  | **VIEW REPORT** | The report is ready | Press it |
  | **RETRY** | The run failed; PokPok is notified by email | Press it to run the report again |

  <p className="pok-tile__note">The same order list also appears on each product's own report page.</p>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // Can't see a report?</div>

  <h2>Reports belong to the account that ordered them</h2>

  <Note>
    A report is tied to the PokPok account that placed the order, not to the browser. If the list is empty, you are probably signed in with a different account: sign out and sign in again the way you did at checkout.
  </Note>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--teal">
  <div className="pok-label">04 // Billing</div>

  <h2>Invoices and payment history</h2>

  <p>On My Reports, under **Manage Billing & Invoices**, press **ACCESS CUSTOMER PORTAL**. The portal, run by Polar, shows your past payments and invoices.</p>

  <a className="pok-button" href="https://pokpok.ai/my-reports">Open My Reports →</a>
</div>
