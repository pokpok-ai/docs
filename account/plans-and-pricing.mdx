## Payment

All payments are processed securely through [Polar.sh](https://polar.sh). Card payments are accepted. After your purchase, you can access your customer portal to view your full payment history and download PDF invoices for any transaction.

## Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions renew automatically each month. You can cancel at any time from your customer portal — there are no long-term contracts, no cancellation fees, and no notice periods. When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

<Note>
  Prices are in EUR. VAT may apply depending on your country.
</Note>
