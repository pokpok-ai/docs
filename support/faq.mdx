<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // About PokPok</div>

  <h2>What it is, and who it is for</h2>

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What is PokPok?">
      PokPok measures the gap between how people and AI read your brand. The free X-RAY scan scores one page on six metrics, three for people and three for AI. The €150 Brand Perception report reads your page across 17 brand perception markers and ends in five Uncomfortable Truths with the fix for each.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Who does the 'people' reading?">
      An AI model, told to read your page the way an impatient, sceptical first-time shopper would. No shopper panel and no survey is involved.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How is this different from asking ChatGPT?">
      ChatGPT generates; PokPok diagnoses. Every report runs your pages through the same 17-marker framework, built for digital commerce brands. Category Perception Truth (PDP) puts your category's 15 leaders side by side on those markers.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why 'The House M.D. for Digital Commerce Brands'?">
      Because a low score is the symptom, not the diagnosis. PokPok runs the diagnostics first, then prescribes. The 5 Uncomfortable Truths in each Brand Perception and Brand Alignment report are the diagnosis, each with its fix.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Does it work for new brands?">
      Yes, and it is best used before you spend on creative. Category Perception Truth (PDP) shows which territories your category's leaders already own and where the empty ground is.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Which categories does PokPok cover?">
      It was built in beauty and skincare and is ready for any DTC category. X-RAY and Brand Perception Truth read any URL you give them.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // X-RAY and credits</div>

  <h2>The free scan</h2>

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="How long does an X-RAY scan take?">
      About 90 seconds: the scanner counts down from "~90s". Your HI score, your POKPOK X-RAY score and the three HI metrics appear when it finishes; the AI score and its three metrics unlock when you sign in.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why is my AI score lower than my HI score?">
      Most brands score around 70 for people and around 40 for AI. For the AI score, a model reads your fetched page the way a search crawler does, on AI Findability, AI Signal Clarity and AI Shelf. Most brands are built for human eyes, then wonder why AI cannot see them.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What is the Roast?">
      A deliberately brutal comedy take on your page, written by AI after the scan. It is entertainment, not a measurement: the scores are the measurement. Signed in, you can share your result on LinkedIn once for **+3 credits**.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="My credits ran out. What now?">
      Scanning anonymously? Sign in for +1. Signed in and never shared? Share once on LinkedIn for +3. Seven is the most you can hold; after that, the next step is a paid report such as Brand Perception Truth (Single URL) at €150. Paid reports do not add credits. Details: [Credits](/account/credits).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Does scanning the same URL again cost a credit?">
      No. If anyone has scanned that URL before, X-RAY returns the stored result and gives your credit back. The stored result is not refreshed, so a page you changed since still shows its old scores. `brand.com/` and `brand.com` count as the same URL; adding `?utm_…` makes a new one.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // Paid reports</div>

  <h2>What €150 gets you</h2>

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="How do I get my report?">
      Diagnose reports are delivered in ~72 hours. You get an email when yours is ready, and it appears on [My Reports](/account/managing-reports).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What is in a Brand Perception report?">
      Your URL read across 17 brand perception markers, a 0–100 POKPOK perception score, and five Uncomfortable Truths. Each truth carries its evidence, why it matters, and the fix, labelled "Hard choice!".
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What does the -geo version add?">
      Brand Perception Truth (Single URL) -geo, also €150, adds the AI reading of the same 17 markers and how far it matches the people reading, marker by marker. It also asks ChatGPT and Gemini six real buyer questions about your category in your market, plus two follow-ups. You see every answer word for word, every brand it names and every link it gives.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I choose which competitors are analysed?">
      For a category report, yes: with the Custom option you name the brands. Diagnose reports analyse no competitors. Brand Perception Truth reads your own URL; Brand Alignment Truth compares your website with your product page.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Where does competitor data come from?">
      From public pages only: product pages, websites and the claims on them. Nothing private is accessed; it is the same content any shopper or AI engine can see.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?">
      No offices, no account teams, no six-week timelines. The agency way costs €5K+ and takes 2–3 weeks; the enterprise way €50K+ and 6–8 weeks. PokPok: €150 and ~72 hours.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>
