<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // About PokPok</div>

  <h2>What it is, and who it is for</h2>

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What is PokPok?">
      PokPok measures the gap between how people and AI read your brand. The €150 Brand Perception Truth report reads one page through both lenses: 5 HI and 5 AI metrics (43 sub-metrics), all 17 brand perception markers read by people and by ChatGPT, and five Uncomfortable Truths with the fix for each. AI Visibility Truth then follows how AI engines name your brand every day.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Who does the 'people' reading?">
      An AI model, told to read your page the way an impatient, sceptical first-time shopper would. No shopper panel and no survey is involved.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="How is this different from asking ChatGPT?">
      ChatGPT generates; PokPok diagnoses. Every report runs your pages through the same 17-marker framework, built for digital commerce brands. Category Perception Truth (PDP) puts your category's 15 leaders side by side on those markers.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why 'The House M.D. for Digital Commerce Brands'?">
      Because a low score is the symptom, not the diagnosis. PokPok runs the diagnostics first, then prescribes. The 5 Uncomfortable Truths in each Brand Perception and Brand Alignment report are the diagnosis, each with its fix.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Does it work for new brands?">
      Yes, and it is best used before you spend on creative. Category Perception Truth (PDP) shows which territories your category's leaders already own and where the empty ground is.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Which categories does PokPok cover?">
      It was built in beauty and skincare and is ready for any DTC category. Brand Perception Truth reads any URL you give it.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Paid reports</div>

  <h2>What €150 gets you</h2>

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="How do I get my report?">
      Diagnose reports are delivered next day. You get an email when yours is ready, and it appears on [My Reports](/account/managing-reports).
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="What is in a Brand Perception report?">
      Your URL read across 17 brand perception markers, a 0–100 POKPOK perception score, and five Uncomfortable Truths. Each truth carries its evidence, why it matters, and the fix, labelled "Hard choice!".
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Does the report include what ChatGPT and Gemini say?">
      Yes. Brand Perception Truth puts ChatGPT's read of all 17 markers next to the people read, with an alignment % per marker. It also asks ChatGPT and Gemini six real buyer questions about your category in your market, plus two follow-ups. You see every answer word for word, every brand it names and every link it gives.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can I choose which competitors are analysed?">
      For a category report, yes: with the Custom option you name the brands. Diagnose reports analyse no competitors. Brand Perception Truth reads your own URL; Brand Alignment Truth compares your website with your product page.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Where does competitor data come from?">
      From public pages only: product pages, websites and the claims on them. Nothing private is accessed; it is the same content any shopper or AI engine can see.
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?">
      No offices, no account teams, no six-week timelines. The agency way costs €5K+ and takes 2–3 weeks; the enterprise way €50K+ and 6–8 weeks. PokPok: €150, delivered next day.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>
