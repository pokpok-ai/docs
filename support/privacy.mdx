  <h2>What is collected, why, and for how long</h2>

  When you use PokPok.ai or contact PokPok, the policy says it may collect:

  | Category | Data |
  |---|---|
  | Contact | First name, last name, email address |
  | Business | Company name, job title, industry |
  | Technical | IP address, browser type, device information, operating system |
  | Usage | Pages visited, features used, time spent on the platform, behavioural patterns |
  | Communication | Messages sent through contact forms or customer support chat |

  | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) | Retention |
  |---|---|---|
  | Responding to contact form enquiries | Art. 6(1)(b) — pre-contractual steps or contract | 2 years from last contact, or until you ask for deletion |
  | Access to the PokPok.ai platform | Art. 6(1)(b) — contract | Duration of the contract + 3 years |
  | Marketing communications | Art. 6(1)(a) — consent, opt-in only, can be withdrawn any time | Until you withdraw consent |
  | Platform analytics and improvement | Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest | Aggregated and anonymised after 26 months |
  | Legal obligations | Art. 6(1)(c) — legal obligation | As Polish law requires (typically 5–6 years) |
  | Fraud prevention and security | Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest | As long as necessary |

  Accounting and tax records are kept 5 years from the end of the fiscal year (Polish Accounting Act).
