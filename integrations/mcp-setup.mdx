<div className="pok-lead">
  Each app gets its **own address** — the Claude one ends in `/private`, the ChatGPT one does not. Paste the right one, sign in with your PokPok email and password, and start a new chat. About two minutes per app.
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // The two addresses</div>

  <h2>Use each address only in its own app</h2>

  | App | Address |
  |---|---|
  | Claude | `https://auth.pokpok.ai/functions/v1/pokpok-mcp-v6/private` |
  | ChatGPT | `https://auth.pokpok.ai/functions/v1/pokpok-mcp-v6` |

  Public reports work without signing in. To see the reports on your own account, sign in with your PokPok email and password when the app asks.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Claude</div>

  <h2>claude.ai in the browser, or the Claude desktop app</h2>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Connectors">
      Open **Settings** → **Connectors** and click **Add custom connector**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Name and address">
      Name: `POKPOK`. Address: `https://auth.pokpok.ai/functions/v1/pokpok-mcp-v6/private`. Click **Add**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Connect and sign in">
      Click **Connect** next to POKPOK. A pokpok.ai page opens: sign in with your PokPok **email and password**, then click **Allow** on the permission page. The page closes and POKPOK shows as connected.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Switch it on in a new chat">
      Start a **new chat**, open the tools menu in the message box and make sure **POKPOK** is switched on.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Tip>
    Sign in with email and password, not the Google button: the Google route ends on the pokpok.ai home page instead of coming back. If that happens, close the tab and click **Connect** again — you are already signed in.
  </Tip>

  If you do not see **Add custom connector**, your Claude workspace owner has to allow custom connectors first.
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // ChatGPT</div>

  <h2>chatgpt.com in the browser</h2>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Add a custom MCP server">
      Go to **chatgpt.com/plugins** and click **Add** (top right) → **Add custom MCP server**. If that option is not there, open **Settings**, turn on **Developer mode** and come back.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Fill in the server">
      Name: `POKPOK`. Address: `https://auth.pokpok.ai/functions/v1/pokpok-mcp-v6`. Authentication: **OAuth** (if it asks).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Create it">
      Accept the warning ("I understand and want to continue") and click **Create**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Connect and sign in">
      A pokpok.ai page opens. Sign in with your PokPok **email and password** (not Google), then click **Allow**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select it in a new chat">
      Start a **new chat** and select **POKPOK** in the message box. If you see older POKPOK entries, select only this one.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <h3>ChatGPT desktop app (the "MCP servers" page)</h3>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Add the server">
      Open the app's **Settings** → **MCP servers** → **Add server**. Name: `pokpok`. Type: **Streamable HTTP**. URL: `https://auth.pokpok.ai/functions/v1/pokpok-mcp-v6`.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save">
      Leave the token and headers empty and click **Save**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Authenticate">
      Back in the list, click **Authenticate** next to `pokpok`, sign in on pokpok.ai and click **Allow**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Switch it on">
      Make sure its switch is **on**, and switch **off** any older POKPOK server in the same list.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">04 // Check it works</div>

  <h2>Ask about a public report first</h2>

  In a new chat with POKPOK selected, ask: **"Show Georganics with POKPOK."** Georganics has a public report, so this works signed in or not. You should see a PokPok page with key numbers, the brand card and the five uncomfortable truths.

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="A plain text answer, no PokPok page">
      Ask again: "Show it as a POKPOK page." Check that POKPOK is selected in this chat.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="&quot;Couldn't identify your brand&quot; when asking about &quot;our brand&quot;">
      You are not signed in. Reconnect POKPOK (Connect in Claude, or the connect step in ChatGPT) and sign in.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="The page looks old or wrong">
      Start a new chat. An open chat can keep an older version of the page.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="PokPok stopped working after you signed out on pokpok.ai">
      Signing out on pokpok.ai also disconnects Claude and ChatGPT. Reconnect and sign in again — and don't press **Sign out** on pokpok.ai while you use PokPok in a chat.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="You want to know which version is live">
      Open `https://auth.pokpok.ai/functions/v1/pokpok-mcp-v6` in a browser: it shows the version and when it was built.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>
