<div className="pok-lead">
  Start each question in a **new chat** with POKPOK selected, and say **"with POKPOK"** so the assistant uses it. Every example below runs on a report or study that exists today.
</div>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">01 // Start with one brand</div>

  <h2>Five questions, five PokPok pages</h2>

  | Ask | What you get in the chat |
  |---|---|
  | Show Georganics with POKPOK. | Key numbers, the brand card, who AI names instead, how people read the page, the five uncomfortable truths, what to fix first |
  | Compare Georganics, banda and Dragon Rouge with POKPOK. | Leaderboard, people against AI, the five AI readings side by side, a scatter |
  | Show the top AI-scored brands with POKPOK. | Leaderboard, people against AI, every score on one line with the average |
  | Who does AI recommend instead of Georganics? (with POKPOK) | Who AI names, with the brand in question in yellow |
  | How is our brand doing? (with POKPOK) | Your own brand's page — needs sign-in |

  Use any brand name or website PokPok holds a report for instead of Georganics. After the page, the assistant answers in words beside it; ask follow-up questions as normal.
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">02 // Go deeper</div>

  <h2>Markers, categories and definitions</h2>

  <AccordionGroup>

<div className="pok-band pok-band--pink">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // Your own brand</div>

  <h2>Sign in, and "our brand" means yours</h2>

  When you ask about "our brand", "us", "my site" or "we" without naming a brand, PokPok opens the report your account ordered. Sign in first, or the answer is "Couldn't identify your brand".

  <div className="pok-grid pok-grid--2">
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>Your reports</h3>
      <p>"What reports do I have? (with POKPOK)" lists what your account bought or was given.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>AI visibility</h3>
      <p>"Which engine names us least: ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI? (with POKPOK)" — for a brand PokPok tracks for your account.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>Cited websites</h3>

    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">

      <p>"Make a shareable PokPok page of our five truths." Returns the page's address.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div className="pok-band">
  <div className="pok-label">04 // How answers behave</div>

  <h2>Stored numbers, quoted as they are</h2>

  | | |
  |---|---|
  | Where numbers come from | PokPok's stored reports; each answer says when the report was measured |
  | Changing report data | Not possible from the chat; a shareable page is the only thing PokPok stores |
  | Rows per table question | 50 by default, at most 200 per call; ask for the next rows to see more |
  | People score and AI score | Two separate 0–100 scales, each reported on its own |
  | A part the report lacks | The answer says so; PokPok does not fill it in |

  <Tip>
    Ask for the reasoning, not just the numbers: "Based on Georganics' five truths, what should we change on the homepage first? (with POKPOK)". The assistant reasons over the stored report in the same reply.
  </Tip>
</div>
