</div>

<div className="pok-band">

  <h2>Public reports for everyone, your own reports after sign-in</h2>

  | You are | You can read |
  |---|---|
  | Not signed in | Every public PokPok report |
  | Signed in with your PokPok email and password | Public reports plus the reports your account ordered; "our brand" opens your own report |

  Tracking how often AI answers name your brand, and making a shareable page, both need sign-in.

  <Note>
    Signing out on pokpok.ai also disconnects PokPok in Claude and ChatGPT. Stay signed in on the site while you use it in a chat.

<div className="pok-band pok-band--yellow">
  <div className="pok-label">03 // How it works</div>

  <h2>Your assistant asks, PokPok answers from the stored report</h2>

  <div className="pok-grid pok-grid--2">
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>PokPok only reads reports</h3>
      <p>Every answer is a stored PokPok report: it says when the report was measured. Nothing in a report can be changed from the chat.</p>
      <p className="pok-tile__note">One exception writes: a shareable page. It stores a new page and returns its address.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>Your assistant does the thinking</h3>
      <p>PokPok's server runs no AI model. Claude or ChatGPT picks the right PokPok tool, reads the numbers and answers in words beside the page.</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>Two scores, kept apart</h3>
      <p>How people read the page and how AI engines read it are two separate 0–100 scales. In the chat, PokPok reports each one on its own.</p>
      <p className="pok-tile__note">See [Scoring](/concepts/scoring).</p>
    </div>
    <div className="pok-tile">
      <h3>Large lists come in pages</h3>
      <p>A table question returns 50 rows by default and at most 200 per call; ask for the next rows to see more.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="The 15 PokPok tools your assistant can call">

      | Tool | What it gives |
      |---|---|
      | `pokpok_show` | The answer drawn as a PokPok page inside the chat |
      | `pokpok_brand_report` | One brand's report: scores, the five people metrics, the five AI metrics, the 17 markers, alignment, truths, fixes, who AI names instead |
      | `pokpok_compare` | Two to ten brands side by side |
      | `pokpok_find` | Which reports and studies exist for a name, website, Amazon listing or category |
      | `pokpok_my_reports` | The reports your account may read (needs sign-in) |
      | `pokpok_catalog` | What PokPok measures: products, category studies, tracked brands, market averages |
      | `pokpok_listing_report` | One Amazon listing against the brand's own website |
      | `pokpok_category_report` | A category study: who leads, what is crowded, what nobody claims, its five truths |
      | `pokpok_visibility` | How often ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI answers name a tracked brand |
      | `pokpok_citations` | Which websites and pages AI engines cite for a tracked brand's questions |
      | `pokpok_ai_answers` | The AI answers themselves, as stored |
      | `pokpok_define` | What each marker, metric and scale means |
      | `pokpok_query` | Rows of the report tables, with filters, for questions the other tools do not cover |
      | `pokpok_fetch` | Any stored part of one report by its id |
      | `pokpok_page` | A shareable web page in PokPok's design, with its address (needs sign-in) |

    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>
